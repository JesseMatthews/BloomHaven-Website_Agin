Blackpool

Home Care in Blackpool

Support at home across Blackpool, from the town centre to Bispham, Layton, Marton and South Shore.

In short

  • We support people in their own homes across FY1 to FY4.
  • Adult social care in Blackpool is run by Blackpool Council, not Lancashire County Council.
  • Blackpool Victoria Hospital is the main hospital, and we can support people coming home from it.
  • Call us for a free home assessment.

Who arranges care in Blackpool

Blackpool is a separate unitary authority. If you want a care needs assessment, a financial assessment or information about direct payments, you ask Blackpool Council, not Lancashire County Council, even though Blackpool is within the county on a map. You can start at Blackpool Council. Many people also arrange and pay for care privately, and you can do both. Our guide to who pays for home care explains the routes.

Coming home from Blackpool Victoria Hospital

Most hospital care for people in Blackpool is provided by Blackpool Victoria Hospital, run by Blackpool Teaching Hospitals NHS Foundation Trust. If you or a relative are due to be discharged, speak to the ward about the plan early, and tell them if you would like to talk to us. Professionals can find out how to refer on our referrals page, and families may find our discharge guide useful.

Where in Blackpool we support people

We cover the FY1, FY2, FY3 and FY4 postcode districts, including the town centre, North Shore, Bispham, Layton, Marton and South Shore. Use the postcode checker if you are not sure. Nearby areas such as Cleveleys (FY5) and Poulton-le-Fylde (FY6) are not on our list yet, but please ask, because we may be able to help or point you to someone who can.

Practical points

Many people in Blackpool live in flats and converted properties, where access, entry systems and parking affect how carers arrive. We talk this through at your assessment and agree how carers will get in, such as with a key safe. Travel through the town can also be slow in busy holiday periods, and we allow for that when planning visit times.

Questions about care in Blackpool

Does Blackpool Council or Lancashire County Council arrange care in Blackpool?
Blackpool Council. It is a separate unitary authority from Lancashire County Council, which covers most of the rest of Lancashire.
Do you cover Cleveleys or Poulton-le-Fylde?
Not at the moment. Our postcode checker lists the areas we currently cover. Get in touch and we will tell you honestly whether we can help.

Getting started

How getting started works

Four steps, at your pace. You can stop at any point.

  4. Care begins

    We introduce your carer first, then review the plan regularly.
