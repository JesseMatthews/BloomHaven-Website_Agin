Complaints and feedback

We want to know what is working and what is not. Complaints help us improve.

Draft for owner approval. The timescales below are proposals. Confirm they match your written complaints policy before publishing.

How to tell us

  • Phone: 07436 125564
  • Email: info@bloomhavencare.co.uk
  • Or speak to your carer or care coordinator. You do not need to use a form.

What happens next

  1. We acknowledge it

    We will confirm we have received your complaint within three working days.

  2. We look into it

    Someone senior who was not involved will investigate, and speak to you to understand what happened.

  3. We reply in writing

    We will explain what we found, what we will change, and apologise where we got something wrong. We aim to do this within 28 days, and will tell you if it will take longer.

If you are not satisfied

You can take your complaint further, whether you pay for your own care or not:

  • The Local Government and Social Care Ombudsman can look at complaints about adult social care providers, including privately funded care
  • If your care is arranged or funded by the council or NHS, you can also complain to them
  • You can share your experience with the Care Quality Commission. The CQC does not resolve individual complaints but uses what it hears in its inspections
