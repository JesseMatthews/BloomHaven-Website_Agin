About us

A new local agency with clear principles

Bloomhaven Care Ltd was incorporated in April 2026. We are new, and we think you deserve to know exactly what that means.

Who we are

Bloomhaven Care is a home care company based in Lancashire. We are a new business, and we are at the stage of building our team and completing our regulatory registration. We would rather tell you that plainly than pretend to a long history we do not have.

What we do have is a clear idea of how care should feel for the person receiving it: listened to, treated with respect, and supported by people who know them.

Our registration status

Bloomhaven Care Ltd is a new company and is not yet registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). In England, personal care at home can only be provided by a CQC-registered provider, so we cannot start delivering care until registration is complete. You are welcome to register your interest now. We will publish our registration details and CQC profile here as soon as they are confirmed. What does this mean for you?

What we stand for

Dignity

Everyone is treated with respect and has the right to make their own choices, including ones we might not make ourselves.

Consistency

Small teams who know you, so you are not starting from scratch every visit.

Honesty

We tell you what we can and cannot do, what it costs, and when something goes wrong.

Partnership

We work with you, your family and the professionals around you.
