For professionals and organisations

Working with Bloomhaven Care

For hospital discharge teams, social workers, GPs, care homes and other organisations who need reliable home care for the people they support.

Please note: we are not yet registered with the CQC and cannot accept referrals for regulated care until we are. You are welcome to introduce yourself now so that we can plan for working together. We will update this page when registration is confirmed. What this means.

In short

  • We provide home care for adults in Preston, Lytham St Annes, Fleetwood and Blackpool.
  • We also plan to supply carers and senior carers to care and nursing homes. Enquiries are welcome.
  • Send an outline through the form or phone us. Please do not put patient details in the form.
  • We tell you quickly and honestly whether we can help, including if we cannot.

Who we work with

  • Hospital discharge and community teams
  • Social workers and local authority teams
  • GPs, practice staff and community nurses
  • Care homes, nursing homes and other providers who need staff, see staffing
  • Brokers and commissioners. Tell us how your process works and we will explain what we can offer.

What we can offer

  • Home care packages for adults who are being discharged from hospital or who need support to stay at home
  • Personal care, dementia care, medication support, overnight care and live-in care
  • A named contact for referrers, and clear communication about starts, changes and concerns
  • Written care plans, daily records and reviews that can be shared with consent

How referrals work

  1. Contact us

    Phone or send the form with a brief outline: where the person lives (postcode district is enough), what support is needed and by when.

  2. We check we can help

    We confirm area, capacity and whether we can safely meet the person’s needs. If we cannot, we will say so quickly so you can look elsewhere.

  3. Assessment and start

    We carry out our own assessment and agree a start date and care plan with the person and their family.

What helps us respond quickly

  • The person’s postcode district, and whether they live alone
  • The type of support needed: personal care, medication, overnight, live-in
  • Number and length of visits, and whether two carers are needed
  • Planned start or discharge date
  • Who is funding the care: self-funded, local authority or NHS
  • Your name, role, organisation and the best way to reach you

Hospital discharge

We understand that discharge dates move quickly. Tell us the planned date and the care needed. We will give you an honest answer about whether we can start in time, and who your contact will be. For the family’s side of the process, see our guide to coming home from hospital.

Staffing for care and nursing homes

As well as home care, we plan to supply staff to care homes, nursing homes and similar services. We are building this alongside our home care service, so please treat this as an invitation to talk, not a promise of availability.

  • Care assistants and senior carers: the roles we expect to supply first
  • Nurses: something we hope to add in time
  • Checks and training: we will explain what we can show you for each person, such as DBS, references, right to work and training, before we agree anything
  • Terms: we will agree rates, cover and responsibilities in writing

If you manage a service and would like to discuss your needs, tell us in the form what roles, shifts and area you have in mind. We will be honest about whether we can help. Please also note our registration status.

Safeguarding and information sharing

We share information only with consent or where the law allows or requires it. See our safeguarding page, quality and safety and privacy policy.

Questions

Can I send patient details through the website form?
No. The form is sent by email through a third-party service, so please do not enter a patient’s name or health details. Send us an outline, or call, and we will take referral details by phone or by your organisation’s secure method.
What happens after I refer?
We confirm we have received it, check whether we can safely meet the person’s needs in their area, and tell you honestly if we cannot. If we can, we arrange our own assessment and agree a start date and care plan.
Can you meet an urgent discharge date?
Tell us the planned date and the care needed, and we will give you an honest answer about whether we can start in time. We will not promise a start we cannot safely deliver.

Professional enquiry

Please do not enter a patient’s name or health details here. This form is sent by email through a third-party service. Give us the outline, and we will take referral details by phone or by your organisation’s secure method.

Please do not include detailed medical or financial information. We will ask for what we need when we speak.

Forms are delivered by FormSubmit, a third-party email service. If you would rather not use a form, call 07436 125564.

Getting started

What the person you refer can expect

The journey for the person and their family, once a referral is accepted.

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message or send an enquiry. We listen first and answer your questions.

  2. Free home assessment

    We visit, meet you and your family, and look at what you would like help with.

  3. Your care plan and quote

    A clear written plan and price. You decide whether to go ahead.

  4. Care begins

    We introduce your carer first, then review the plan regularly.
