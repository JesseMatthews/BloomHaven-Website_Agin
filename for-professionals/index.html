For professionals and organisations

Working with Bloomhaven Care

For hospital discharge teams, social workers, GPs, care homes and other organisations who need reliable home care for the people they support.

Please note: we are not yet registered with the CQC and cannot accept referrals for regulated care until we are. You are welcome to introduce yourself now so that we can plan for working together. We will update this page when registration is confirmed.

What we can offer

  • Home care packages for adults who are being discharged from hospital or who need support to stay at home
  • Personal care, dementia care, medication support, overnight care and live-in care
  • A named contact for referrers, and clear communication about starts, changes and concerns
  • Written care plans, daily records and reviews that can be shared with consent

How referrals work

  1. Contact us

    Phone or send the form with a brief outline: who the person is, where they live, what support is needed and by when.

  2. We check we can help

    We confirm area, capacity and whether we can safely meet the person’s needs. If we cannot, we will say so quickly.

  3. Assessment and start

    We carry out our own assessment and agree a start date and care plan.

Staffing and other enquiries

If you run a care home or other service and would like to discuss supporting your work, please tell us what you need in the form. We will be honest about whether it is something we can do.

Safeguarding and information sharing

We share information only with consent or where the law allows or requires it. See our safeguarding page and privacy policy.

Professional enquiry

Please do not include detailed medical or financial information. We will ask for what we need when we speak.
