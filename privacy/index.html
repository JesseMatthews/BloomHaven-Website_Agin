Privacy Policy

Last updated 16 November 2026.

Draft for owner and adviser review. This reflects how the website works. Before relying on it, check it against your actual data practices, register with the ICO if required, and have it reviewed by someone qualified.

Who we are

Bloomhaven Care Ltd (company number 17172482) is the controller of personal information described here. Contact us at info@bloomhavencare.co.uk or 07436 125564.

What we collect and why

  • Enquiry forms: your name, email, phone, postcode and message, to reply to you. Our legal basis is legitimate interests, or taking steps at your request before a contract.
  • Careers enquiries: the details you provide, to consider your interest in working with us.
  • Phone, email and WhatsApp: whatever you share with us, to respond.

Please do not put detailed health or financial details on a form. We will ask for what we need when we speak. If we later provide care, we will give you a separate notice about how we handle care records.

Who we share it with

Form messages are delivered by FormSubmit (formsubmit.co), which passes them to our email inbox. Our email and phone providers also process messages. If you contact us on WhatsApp, Meta processes the messages. We do not sell personal information. We share it where the law requires, or to protect someone from harm.

This website

This website does not use advertising or analytics cookies. Fonts are hosted on our own server. The “view on Google Maps” links take you to Google only if you click them.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that do not lead to care are kept for up to 12 months. Careers enquiries are kept for up to 6 months unless you ask us to remove them sooner. (Owner to confirm retention periods.)

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct, delete or restrict the use of your personal information, and to object to its use. Contact us at info@bloomhavencare.co.uk. You can also complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk.
