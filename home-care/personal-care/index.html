Daily living

Personal care at home

Help with the most personal parts of the day, given quietly and respectfully, the way you want it done.

Needing help with washing or dressing is a big thing to ask for. It is also one of the most common reasons people look for home care, and it is something carers do every day. We start from your habits, not ours: whether you prefer a bath or a shower, a morning wash or an evening one, what you like to wear, and who you are comfortable having in the room.

What this can include

  • Washing, bathing and showering
  • Dressing and undressing, including help with buttons, stockings or support stockings
  • Toileting and continence care
  • Oral hygiene and denture care
  • Skin care and looking out for changes or sore areas
  • Shaving, hair care and grooming
  • Getting up in the morning and going to bed at night
  • Support to keep your own independence, doing what you can and helping with what you cannot

Who it helps

  • People recovering from illness, a fall or a hospital stay
  • People living with arthritis, Parkinson’s, stroke or frailty
  • People whose family are providing care and need a break or extra support
  • People who simply want a hand, rather than moving out of their own home

How it works

Every package begins with a conversation and an assessment at home, then a written care plan that says what help you want, how, and when. Where possible you will see the same small group of carers, so you are not explaining yourself to a new person every visit. We review the plan regularly and whenever things change.

Things to bear in mind

Visits can be short (for example a morning call) or longer, and can be a few days a week or daily. Some people want a woman or man carer for personal care. Tell us and we will do our best to match this.

Questions

Can I choose a male or female carer?
You can tell us your preference at assessment and we will do our best to honour it. We will be honest if we cannot match it on a particular visit.
What if I am embarrassed?
Carers help people with personal care every day. They are trained to be discreet and matter-of-fact, and to protect your privacy, for example by keeping you covered and closing doors.
How long is a visit?
It depends on what you need. We agree this with you at assessment rather than working to a fixed slot.

Getting started

How getting started works

Four steps, at your pace. You can stop at any point.

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message or send an enquiry. We listen first and answer your questions.

  2. Free home assessment

    We visit, meet you and your family, and look at what you would like help with.

  3. Your care plan and quote

    We introduce your carer first, then review the plan regularly.
