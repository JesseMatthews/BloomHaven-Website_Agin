Live-in care at home

One-to-one support day and night, without leaving the home you know.

With live-in care a carer stays in your home, usually for a set period, so that help is on hand throughout the day and available at night. It can be a good fit if someone needs more help than visits can give, but does not want to move to a care home. It also suits couples who want to stay together.

What this can include

  • Personal care, mobility and continence support
  • Meals, shopping and light housework
  • Medication prompts or support, where this is agreed
  • Company, outings and appointments
  • Support through the night where it is needed, which may be sleep-in or waking nights depending on need
  • A routine that suits you, with the carer’s own breaks planned in
  • Clear updates for family

Who it helps

  • People who need frequent help through the day and some help at night
  • People with advanced frailty or dementia who are settled at home
  • Couples where one needs care and both want to stay in the same house
  • People coming home from hospital who need intensive support at first

How it works

We begin with a detailed assessment, including your home, so that we can see if a live-in carer can work there. A carer will need their own room, reasonable rest breaks and enough sleep. Carers normally work for a set period and are then covered by a colleague, so continuity is planned in rather than left to chance.

Things to bear in mind

Live-in care is a significant commitment. The cost is higher than visiting care, but for some people it compares well with a care home, particularly for couples. We are open about cost and how it is made up. Please see our guide to paying for care.

Questions

Does the carer need their own room?
Yes. A live-in carer needs a private bedroom and reasonable time off. We will talk this through at assessment.
What if the person needs help every night?
If someone needs regular help overnight, a waking-night arrangement or an additional carer may be needed. We will agree what is safe and fair for everyone.
Is it better than a care home?
It depends on the person. Live-in care keeps people in their own home with one-to-one attention. A care home may suit people who need nursing care or who would benefit from company. We will give you an honest view.
