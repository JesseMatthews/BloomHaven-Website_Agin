Night-time support

Overnight care at home

Nights can be the hardest part of caring. A carer in the house can make them safer and calmer for everyone.

Some people feel anxious or confused at night, need help to the toilet, or are at risk of falling when alone. Family carers often go without sleep for months. Overnight care gives someone safe cover through the night.

What this can include

  • Waking-night carers who stay awake and check on you throughout the night
  • Sleep-in carers who are in the house and available if you need help
  • Help with toileting, repositioning and continence care
  • Reassurance if you wake anxious or disorientated
  • Medication support, where agreed
  • A calm start to the morning, with a handover to family or the morning visit

Who it helps

  • People who are at risk of falls at night
  • People with dementia whose sleep is disturbed
  • People with pain or conditions that need care at night
  • Family carers who need a regular night off, or a break after a hospital stay

How it works

We agree with you what level of night-time help is needed. A sleep-in is for people who need help only occasionally. A waking night is for people who need regular attention. Some people use us one or two nights a week for respite; others need more regular cover.

Things to bear in mind

Night care is staffed and priced differently from daytime care. We will explain how, before you decide.

Questions

What is the difference between sleep-in and waking night?
A sleep-in carer sleeps at the property and can be woken if needed, usually only once or twice. A waking-night carer is awake and active throughout the night.
Can I book one-off nights?
Often yes, subject to availability and an assessment. Please call us.
