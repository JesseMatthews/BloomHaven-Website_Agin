Home care services

Home care in Lancashire

Whether you need an hour a day or someone living in, we will build care around you. Here is what we offer and how it works.

In short

  • We offer seven kinds of home care, from an hour of company to live-in support.
  • You can use one service or combine several, and change them as your needs change.
  • Every service starts with a free home assessment, with no obligation.
  • Not sure what you need? Call us and we will help you work it out.
Daily living

Personal care

Respectful help with washing, dressing, continence and getting up or going to bed, in your own routine.

Learn more →Specialist

Dementia care

Patient, consistent support for people living with dementia, and practical help for the family around them.

Learn more →24-hour support

Live-in care

A carer who lives in your home, giving continuous support as an alternative to moving into a care home.

Learn more →Night-time support

Overnight care

Sleep-in or waking night support so that you, and your family, can rest with peace of mind.

Learn more →Wellbeing

Companionship

Regular company, conversation and a reason to get out, to ease loneliness and keep life interesting.

Learn more →Health

Medication support

Prompting and support so that medicines are taken correctly, with careful records and clear communication.

Learn more →Home & mobility

Everyday home support

Meals, light housework, shopping and safe movement around the home, the practical things that keep life going.

Not sure what you need?

Start with a conversation

Most people do not know exactly what care they need. A free assessment at home helps us agree it together. There is no obligation.

Arrange an assessmentPaying for care

What an assessment covers

  • What you want to keep doing yourself
  • Where you would like help, and when
  • Health conditions, medicines and equipment
  • Your home and any risks in it
  • Who is involved: family, friends, GP, social worker
  • Cost, in writing, before you decide

Talk to us about care

Tell us what you are looking for. A real person will reply within one working day, and there is no pressure to go any further.
