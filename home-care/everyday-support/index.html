Home & mobility

Everyday home support

The practical help that lets you stay at home: food on the table, a clean and safe house, and a steady arm when you need it.

Sometimes the difficulty is not personal care but the everyday jobs: cooking, laundry, changing the bed, getting to the shops or getting out of a chair safely. This support can be offered on its own or alongside other care.

What this can include

  • Planning and preparing meals, including for special diets, and making sure you eat and drink enough
  • Shopping, collecting prescriptions and putting shopping away
  • Light housework: tidying, washing up, laundry, changing beds, emptying bins
  • Help to move safely: getting up from a chair or bed, walking with aids, using stairs
  • Helping you use equipment that has been supplied to you, once a carer is trained to do so
  • Noticing hazards in the home and telling you or your family

Who it helps

  • People who find cooking or cleaning difficult
  • People recovering from surgery or illness
  • People who are less steady on their feet and worried about falls
  • People who need a regular check that all is well

How it works

We agree a routine with you. If someone needs hoists or other equipment for moving and handling, we will assess this first and ensure carers are trained on that equipment before they use it. We may also ask for advice from your occupational therapist or physiotherapist.

Things to bear in mind

Housekeeping here means light, everyday help, not deep cleaning or heavy gardening. We will tell you honestly if a task is outside what we do.

Questions

Can you cook specific meals?
Yes, within reason. Tell us what you like, any cultural or dietary needs, and any advice from a dietitian or doctor.
Do you provide equipment like hoists?
We do not supply equipment. Where equipment is already provided for you, carers can use it once trained. A health or social care professional can arrange an assessment.

Getting started

How getting started works

Four steps, at your pace. You can stop at any point.

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message or send an enquiry. We listen first and answer your questions.

  2. Free home assessment

    We visit, meet you and your family, and look at what you would like help with.

  3. Your care plan and quote

    A clear written plan and price. You decide whether to go ahead.

  4. Care begins

    We introduce your carer first, then review the plan regularly.
