Bloomhaven Care Ltd is a new company, incorporated in April 2026. We are not yet registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC), so we cannot provide personal care yet. This page explains what that means for you, and how to check any care provider for yourself.

What is CQC registration?

The Care Quality Commission is the independent regulator of health and social care in England. Providers who carry out “regulated activities”, which include personal care at home, must be registered with the CQC. Registration means the CQC has checked that the provider meets basic requirements, including having a suitable registered manager.

What registration does not mean

Registration is not a rating. Ratings (Outstanding, Good, Requires improvement or Inadequate) come after the CQC inspects a service, and a new provider may not have been inspected yet. A good provider will tell you which stage it is at, and will not describe itself in a way that suggests more.

What this means for you right now

  • We can talk to you, answer questions and explain how we would work
  • You can register your interest, and we will tell you when we are registered
  • We will not begin personal care before registration is complete
  • If you need care urgently, please speak to your council or GP, or look for a registered provider, using the steps below

How to check any care provider

  1. Search the CQC website

    Go to the CQC website and search for the provider or the location by name.

  2. Check the details

    Look for the registered location, the regulated activity “personal care”, and the registered manager.

  3. Read the report and its date

    Check the rating, when it was awarded and what inspectors found. Old reports may no longer reflect the service.
