Questions and answers

Frequently asked questions

If you cannot find your answer here, please call or message us.

Arranging care

How do I arrange home care?
Call, message or use the enquiry form. We talk first, then visit you at home for a free assessment, write a care plan and quote, and agree a start date.
Is the assessment really free?
Yes, and there is no obligation to go ahead.
Can I choose my carer?
We match carers according to your needs and preferences, and will always introduce you before regular visits begin. If it is not working, tell us and we will change it.
Can care change as my needs change?
Yes. Your plan is reviewed regularly and any time something changes. Visits can be added, reduced or changed.
How quickly can care start?
It depends on what is needed and our capacity. Tell us how urgent it is and we will give you an honest answer.

Costs and funding

How much does it cost?
It depends on the visits, hours and times of day. You will get a written quote after the assessment. See paying for care.
Can the council or NHS pay?
Possibly. It depends on your needs and finances. See paying for care for how to ask.

Safety and regulation

Are you regulated by the CQC?
We are a new company and are not yet registered. We cannot deliver personal care until we are. We will publish our registration details here as soon as they are confirmed.
Are your carers checked?
Carers have enhanced DBS checks, references and identity and right-to-work checks before they visit anyone.
How do I complain?
Please see complaints and feedback.

Working with us

Do I need experience to work in care?
No. We provide induction and training.
How do I apply?
Use the form on our careers page.

Talk to us about care

Tell us what you are looking for. A real person will reply within one working day, and there is no pressure to go any further.
