Quality and safety

How we approach quality and safety

The Care Quality Commission judges care on five questions. This is how we plan to meet each one, and how you can hold us to it.

Our registration status

Bloomhaven Care Ltd is a new company and is not yet registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). In England, personal care at home can only be provided by a CQC-registered provider, so we cannot start delivering care until registration is complete. You are welcome to register your interest now. We will publish our registration details and CQC profile here as soon as they are confirmed.

A website cannot prove that care is good. Only inspection and the experience of the people we support can do that. What we can do is tell you plainly what we commit to, and where to check our regulator’s findings. You can find any registered provider and its reports on the CQC website.

Safe

Are people protected from abuse and avoidable harm?

  • Staff are recruited safely: identity and right-to-work checks, enhanced DBS checks, references and a full employment history
  • Every client has a written risk assessment for the home and for their needs, and carers are told about the risks before they visit
  • Safeguarding training for all staff, and a clear route to raise concerns, including whistleblowing
  • Medicines are handled following NICE guidance, with records at every visit
  • Infection prevention and control training, and personal protective equipment
  • Incidents and near misses are recorded and learned from

Effective

Does care and support achieve good outcomes?

  • An assessment before care starts, covering needs, preferences and goals
  • Care plans written with you, and reviewed regularly and when things change
  • Induction and training following the Care Certificate standards, with competence checks
  • Regular supervision and observation of carers at work
  • Working with GPs, district nurses, therapists and others, with your consent
  • Following the Mental Capacity Act 2005 and supporting people to make their own decisions

Caring

Do staff involve and treat people with compassion, kindness, dignity and respect?

  • We ask what matters to you and put it in the plan, in your words
  • Consistent carers, so people are known and not rushed
  • Privacy, dignity and choice protected in every visit
  • Respect for religion, culture, gender and identity
  • Families kept informed, as much as the person wants

Responsive

Are services organised so that they meet people’s needs?

  • Care that fits around your routine, not the other way around
  • Plans reviewed when things change, such as after a fall or hospital stay
  • A named contact, and an out-of-hours number for people we support
  • A simple, well-publicised complaints process, and we learn from what people tell us

Well-led

Does leadership assure high-quality, person-centred care, support learning and promote a fair and open culture?

  • A registered manager, once approved by the CQC, accountable for the quality of care
  • Regular audits of records, medicines, care plans and visits
  • Feedback sought from people we support, families and staff, and acted on
  • A duty of candour: we will be open and honest when something goes wrong
  • Policies kept up to date, and staff supported to follow them
