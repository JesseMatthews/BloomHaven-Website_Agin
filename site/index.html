Getting started

From first call to first visit

You do not need to know exactly what you need. Tell us what is happening and we will help you work it out.

Ask about care

  1. Talk to us

    Call, message or send an enquiry. We will listen and answer your questions.

  2. Free home assessment

    We visit, meet you and your family and look at your home, your routine and what you would like help with.

  3. Your care plan

    We write a clear plan and quote. You decide whether to go ahead.

  4. Care begins, and keeps being reviewed

    We introduce your carer before the first visit and review the plan regularly.

Quality and safety

Held to the standards that matter

Care in England is judged on five questions. We plan, train and check against them, and we say plainly where we are in the process.

Our registration status

Bloomhaven Care Ltd is a new company and is not yet registered with the Care Quality Commission (CQC). In England, personal care at home can only be provided by a CQC-registered provider, so we cannot start delivering care until registration is complete. You are welcome to register your interest now. We will publish our registration details and CQC profile here as soon as they are confirmed.

Our people

Common questions

Answers before you ask

How do I arrange home care?
Call us, message us or fill in the enquiry form. We will have a first conversation, then visit you at home for a free assessment, with no obligation. From that we write a care plan, agree a start date and introduce your carer.
What areas do you cover?
We cover Lancashire, including Preston, Lytham St Annes, Fleetwood and Blackpool. Use the postcode checker on the areas page. If you are just outside, ask us.
How much does home care cost?
It depends on the type of care, the number and length of visits and whether you need nights. We give you a written quote after your assessment. Please see paying for care for how funding works.
Are you regulated by the CQC?
We are a new company and are not yet registered with the Care Quality Commission. We cannot deliver personal care until we are. We will publish our registration details on this website as soon as they are confirmed.
